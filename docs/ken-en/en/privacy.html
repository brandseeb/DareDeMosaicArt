Privacy Policy

KEN EN: Animal Chain Puzzle / 犬猿の仲
Effective and updated: October 1, 2026

brandseeb (the developer) provides this app and describes its handling of information below.

1. Information in the app

No account is required. Puzzle completion records, hint usage, the daily unlock date, tutorial status and audio settings are stored on your device. The developer does not transmit or collect these game records or settings on a developer-operated server. Device backups may include this data depending on your Apple service settings.

The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not request camera, photo library, location, contacts or microphone access.

2. In-app purchases

Purchases and restoration use Apple's App Store and StoreKit. The app uses verified transaction information from Apple, such as the product identifier, purchase validity and revocation status, to determine access to Chapter 4. The developer does not receive card numbers, payment account details or your Apple Account password. Apple's processing is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Support requests

Information and contact details you submit through the support form are used to respond, assist you and investigate problems. Do not submit passwords or payment card details. Support information is retained as needed to resolve requests or as required by law, then deleted when no longer needed. You can request access to or deletion of support information through the same form.

The form uses Google Forms. Google's handling of information when you open or submit the form is also governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Public website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertisements, our own analytics or external fonts. GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses when you visit. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

5. Sharing

The developer does not sell support information or use it for advertising. It is shared only as needed with support service providers, with your consent, or as required by law.

6. Deleting local data

Deleting the app removes its local records and settings. Offloading an app may retain its data. Manage backups separately in your device or Apple service settings. Deleting the app does not remove Apple's purchase history; you can restore the purchase after reinstalling.

7. Children and updates

Minors should have a parent or guardian's permission for purchases and support requests. We may update this policy as features or legal requirements change. The updated policy and date will appear here.

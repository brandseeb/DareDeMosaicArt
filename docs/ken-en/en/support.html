Frequently asked questions

I bought Chapter 4 but it is locked

Sign into the App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase, connect to the internet and choose Restore Purchases in Settings or the Chapter 4 purchase screen. Pending purchases unlock after approval. Contact us if this does not help.

Is this a subscription?

No. Chapter 4 includes all 50 puzzles for a one-time purchase. Future separate packs are not included.

Does restoring purchases restore my progress?

No. It restores access to Chapter 4. Progress is stored locally without our own server sync. Backup and device transfer behavior depends on your Apple service settings.

When does the daily challenge change?

Complete all 20 Chapter 1 puzzles to unlock it. It changes at midnight in your device's local time. Past puzzles since unlocking remain available in History.

Why is the hint unavailable?

Each puzzle allows one hint, including retries. Hints are unavailable on one-move puzzles.

How do I request a refund?

Use Apple's Report a Problem page. Apple determines eligibility.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove local records. Request deletion of support information through the form. See the Privacy Policy.
